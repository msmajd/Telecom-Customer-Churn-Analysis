# Telecom-Customer-Churn-Analysis


## Project Overview
Customer churn is a critical challenge for subscription-based businesses. This project analyzes customer churn behavior in a telecom company to identify key drivers of churn and provide data-driven retention strategies.

The analysis focuses on understanding **who is leaving, why they are leaving, and which customer segments should be prioritized for retention**.

---

## Business Problem
The company is experiencing a high customer churn rate, impacting revenue stability and long-term growth. Management needs clear insights into churn drivers to implement effective retention strategies.

---

## Tools & Technologies
- **SQLite & SQL** – Data modeling and churn analysis
- **Python (Pandas)** – Data preparation and validation
- **Power BI** – Interactive dashboards and visualization

---

## Key Insights
- Customers on **month-to-month contracts** have the highest churn rate (~21%)
- **Early tenure customers (0–6 months)** are the most likely to churn
- Customers without **Tech Support or Online Security** show significantly higher churn
- Long-term contracts and support services are strong retention factors

---

## Recommended Actions
- Incentivize customers to switch to long-term contracts
- Improve onboarding and engagement during the first 6 months
- Offer support services to high-risk customer segments
- Deploy targeted retention campaigns for at-risk customers

---

## Dashboard
The Power BI dashboard provides interactive analysis of:
- Churn rate by contract type
- Churn by tenure groups
- Service usage impact on churn
- High-risk customer segments

---

## Conclusion
This project demonstrates an end-to-end analytics workflow that transforms raw data into actionable business insights, supporting strategic decision-making and customer retention efforts.
